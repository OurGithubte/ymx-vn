Product Applications

Mobile Phone

The set of precision die-cut materials that make up a smartphone, from the frame to thermal and protective layers.

← Back to Products & Applications

Mobile phones have the highest component density among the company's die-cutting applications. Each phone requires dozens of precision die-cut parts, accurate to the millimeter, for bonding, fixing, heat dissipation, dust-proofing and protecting internal components.

Featured Die-Cut Materials & Components

Double-Sided Tape

Bonds the screen and back cover of the phone.

Graphite Sheet

Dissipates heat from heat-generating components such as the processor and battery.

Dust-Proof Net

Mesh fitted at the speaker/microphone area to keep out dust.

Protective Film

Protects the screen/housing surface during assembly and transport.

Poron Foam

Shock-absorbing cushion that seals gaps inside the device.

Kapton Tape

High-heat-resistant tape used to secure the flexible printed circuit (FPC) inside the device.
