Product Applications

Backlight Module

The precision die-cut optical layers that make up an LCD backlight module.

← Back to Products & Applications

A backlight module is a stack of optical layers, each performing a specific function: light guiding, diffusion, brightness enhancement, reflection and light blocking. The dimensional accuracy of each die-cut layer directly affects the display quality of the screen.

Featured Die-Cut Materials & Components

Light-Blocking Tape

Prevents light from leaking out of the backlight module.

Reflective Sheet

Reflects light back to improve lighting efficiency.

Diffusion Sheet

Diffuses light evenly across the screen surface.

Brightness Enhancement Film

Enhances brightness level and light uniformity.

Protective Film

Protects the surface of optical layers during assembly.

Foam

Shock-absorbing cushion that secures the layers within the backlight module.
