Product Applications

Notebook Computer

Die-cut materials used for assembly, heat dissipation and product identification in laptops.

← Back to Products & Applications

Inside a laptop is a complex system of components that must be fixed, insulated, thermally managed and protected in the correct position. Precision die-cut products help ensure each component layer is neatly assembled, dissipates heat effectively, and meets brand identification standards.

Featured Die-Cut Materials & Components

PC/PP/PET Sheet

Die-cut technical plastic sheet used for decoration and scratch protection of the laptop surface.

Conductive Foam/Cloth Tape

Conductive material providing EMI shielding for the circuit board.

Label

Labels identifying configuration (chip, operating system, licensing certification, etc.).

Copper/Aluminum Foil

Metal foil providing thermal conduction, electrical conduction and shielding for circuit areas.

Thermal Conductive Sheet

Thermal pad placed between components and the heat sink.

Double-Sided Tape

Secures component layers inside the laptop.
