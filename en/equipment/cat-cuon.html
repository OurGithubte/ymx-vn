Roll Cutting Machine

Equipment using a circular blade to cut roll material to a set width and quantity.

← Back to Equipment

The roll cutting machine (model FCA-1600) uses a circular blade to cut roll material into shorter rolls/lengths according to the width and quantity specified in the production order. It is controlled via an HMI touchscreen and includes an auxiliary control panel to assist alignment when installing a new blade or changing the cutting width.

Main Structure

  • Main control cabinet (HMI + control buttons)
  • Circular blade assembly and clamping zone - high-risk area
  • Auxiliary control panel used for alignment
  • Material shaft and support head

Reference Technical Specifications

ModelFCA-1600
Power7.5 kW
Voltage380V
Machine dimensions (LxWxH)3,460 x 1,380 x 1,710 mm

⚠️ Operating Safety Notes

The sharp, high-speed rotating circular blade can cut hands or eject material/blade fragments; the rotating roll shaft can catch sleeves, hair or gloves causing entanglement. Guards must be fully in place, hands must not be inserted, power must be off when adjusting the blade, and cut-resistant gloves used only when appropriate.
